Save what you find on Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok.

InstaSpace downloads reels, posts, stories, saved posts and whole profiles, your X Bookmarks, YouTube videos and TikTok videos to your own computer or phone, in tidy folders, and never downloads the same thing twice.

What it saves

Public links work without an account. Saved posts, stories and profiles use your own signed-in session.

Instagram

  • Reels, posts and carousels from links
  • Saved posts and collections, with optional un-save after download
  • Stories and highlights
  • Whole profiles: posts, Reels tab or Tagged
  • Watchlist: chosen accounts checked automatically

X

  • Your Bookmarks, photos at original size and best-quality video
  • Remove bookmarks once they're saved
  • Posts from links

YouTube

  • MP4 up to 4K, M4A or MP3
  • Whole playlists and channels, each in its own folder
  • No account needed

TikTok

  • Videos from links, without the watermark when available
  • Your Favorites and Liked videos (Mac)
  • Not available where TikTok is blocked, such as India

Install

Free downloads, straight from this site's GitHub releases.

Mac

  1. Open InstaSpace-1.3.3.dmg and drag the app to Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app and choose Open.
Download for Mac

Windows

  1. Run InstaSpace-Setup-1.3.0.exe.
  2. If Windows warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.
Download for Windows

Android

  1. Download InstaSpace-1.3.0.apk on your phone and open it.
  2. Allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
